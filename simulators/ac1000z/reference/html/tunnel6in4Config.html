    6RD Connection

    What should be noticed when configuring 6RD connection?

    1. The MTU of 6RD is suggest to meet the condition: MTU <= the MTU of underlying WAN connection - 20.
    e.g. if the MTU of underlying WAN connection is 1400, the MTU of 6RD is suggest to be less than or equal to 1380.
    2. The length of "6RD Prefix" and "IPV4 Mask Length" have relations as below: 32 - "IPV4 Mask Length" + "6RD Prefix Length" <= 64.
    3. "6RD Border Relay Address" can not be "0.0.0.0".

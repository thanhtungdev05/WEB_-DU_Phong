Advanced Settings
You can customize advanced Wi-Fi settings and use these settings to fine-tune your Wi-Fi network for better performance:
  • Bandwidth controls channel width for speed or interference.
  • Channel selects the Wi-Fi frequency to avoid interference.
  • Enabling DFS (Dynamic Frequency Selection) channels can help improve Wi-Fi performance in 5Hz band, for compatible devices. Some older devices may not support DFS channels and will be unable to connect on 5GHz when DFS channels are in use. If you experience any problems with devices not connecting on 5GHz, you may wish to Disable the use of DFS channels.
REMINDER
  • Changes to radio or SSID settings may temporarily disrupt your Wi-Fi connection.
  • When DFS is enabled, the actual bandwidth and channel may differ from the configured settings.
